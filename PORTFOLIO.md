# Relay CRM

**Category:** Business software · CRM · Interactive portfolio demo

**Status:** Functional demo / ongoing portfolio project

Relay is a CRM for small service teams that want a clearer view of their relationships, opportunities, and next steps. I designed and built a connected sales workspace that takes a prospect from the first conversation to a project handoff.

The demo includes a daily follow-up view, a six-stage sales pipeline, linked company and contact records, task planning, searchable activity histories, and carefully reviewed CSV imports. Each visitor gets a fictional workspace saved in their own browser, so they can explore the real workflows without creating an account.

**What this project demonstrates**

- Designing business software around practical, connected workflows.
- Building responsive tables, accessible record drawers, and a pipeline with drag-and-drop and keyboard alternatives.
- Calculating useful metrics from live records, with clear definitions.
- Validating data and preserving relationships through imports, archive/restore, and undo.
- Testing the full experience across desktop, tablet, and mobile layouts.
